This is **placeholder content**, visible only in development. It exists so the
Insights layout can be built and reviewed before any real article is written.
The brand rule is firm — nothing invented ships to the live site — so this post
carries `draft: true` and never reaches production.

## What this section is for

Insights is where WIZAG shares practical perspective on the work: enterprise
transformation, ERP, AI and operational excellence. When real articles are ready,
they replace files like this one and the section is switched on.

A real post can lean on a few things the layout already gives it:

- Clean, readable long-form typography drawn straight from the design tokens
- Embedded diagrams — the same animated components used across the site
- Categories, tags and an estimated reading time for wayfinding

> The measure of a good article is whether a busy operations director finishes it.
> Everything here is tuned for that reader.

## How to go live

Replace this placeholder with a real piece, set `draft: false` in its frontmatter,
and remove `/insights` from the noindex list in [astro.config.mjs](/insights). Until
then, the whole section stays quietly dormant.
